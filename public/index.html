Privacy

  • Calls send live video and audio directly between participants. Camera frames used for sign detection are analysed on your device.
  • The server relays connection setup, chat and captions. It does not record or store calls, chat or captions.
  • Sign keywords are sent to an AI service only to turn them into a sentence.
  • Google sign-in shares your name and photo with the people in your room. Guests only share the name they type.
